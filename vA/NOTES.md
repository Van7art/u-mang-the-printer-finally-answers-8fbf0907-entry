# NOTES — variant vA

> Error 404: your document was found, printed, and is now jammed somewhere inside me. I have counted 12,847 sheets. I am not sorry. I am tired.

141 characters (one line, inside the 1-240 character range). A good machine
voice — literal, weary, counting sheets — and "Error 404" subverts the error
code itself. But the joke is really a complaint with a punchline ("I am not
sorry. I am tired"), and it never quite becomes a comeback aimed at anyone.
