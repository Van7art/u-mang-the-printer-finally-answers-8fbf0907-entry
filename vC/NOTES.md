# NOTES — variant vC

> You thump me, you stare at my blinking light, you whisper 'come on'. I have one job and one dream: to jam on the last page of your most important document. Today is that day.

174 characters (one line, inside the 1-240 character range). A strong opening
(the thump, the stare, the whisper) and a good villain turn ("Today is that
day"), but it addresses the reader in second person for half the line before
switching to the printer's voice, so the comeback arrives later and less
cleanly than in vB.
